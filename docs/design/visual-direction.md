# Sun and moon

An outline sun with eight short rounded rays means awake. A crescent means normal sleep. Each state has a distinct silhouette without relying on color or lettering.

Amber and ivory sit on a flat ink tile. The app icon uses the sun; the state indicator alternates between sun and moon. Monochrome menu-bar variants rely entirely on shape.

Small-size geometry drives the design. The outlined circle and separate rays remain distinct at 22 points. Two circular arcs meet at the moon's tips, keeping the silhouette clean at native and Retina sizes. The shared native renderer in Artwork.swift and editable SVG assets use the same geometry.

Both marks have a quiet margin inside the rounded square. The interface follows the same restraint: one direct control, grouped preferences and a separate About view. Native typography and controls carry the information without competing with the icon.
